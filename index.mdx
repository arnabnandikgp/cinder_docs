Cinder gives you a private trading account for accessing Solana perp venues.

Cinder tracks your balance, positions and trading results separately, while executing through shared accounts on supported venues. Your account reflects your own trading results; it is not a share in an investment pool.

Your orders and account data are processed inside a confidential enclave. Venues see Cinder's pooled execution, rather than a separate venue account for each customer. Deposits and withdrawals remain visible on Solana.
