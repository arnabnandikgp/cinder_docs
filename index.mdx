Cinder separates your trading account from the public venue account used for execution. Your balances, positions, orders and trading history stay inside the confidential service. External venues receive Cinder's pooled execution flow.

You retain an individual recorded entitlement—not shares in a trading pool. Actual fills, funding and costs determine your account economics.

Start with [availability](/status), then the [SDK quickstart](/quickstart). Pacifica is the first integration; this reference covers the implemented, locally tested interface. It is not an invitation to deposit real funds.

- [Account model](/guides/accounts): pooled execution and individual entitlements.
- [API reference](/api/overview): signed commands, private reads and WebSocket updates.
