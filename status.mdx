Cinder provides private perpetuals accounts with pooled execution on supported Solana venues. Pacifica is the first venue integration.

| Area | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| Account access | Wallet-authenticated owners and permissioned trading agents |
| Trading | Bounded GTC, add-liquidity-only and immediate-or-cancel orders; cancellation; selectable leverage up to a market cap |
| Account data | Private balances, positions, operations, orders, fills, funding and history |
| Integrations | The same signed commands over HTTP and WebSocket, plus private WebSocket updates |
| Funds | Solana deposits, collateral routing and withdrawals to the account's configured recipient |
| Protection | Margin and pool-risk limits, policy-defined house protection and conditional ADL restoration |
| Recovery | Operator-assisted settlement followed by independently verifiable, owner-signed claims |

Shared execution does not make your account an investment-pool share. Your trading results are recorded individually; ordinary trading losses are not automatically shared with other customers.

## Interface boundaries

The documented interface covers perpetuals. It does not expose spot trading, isolated-margin accounts, trigger orders, TP/SL, atomic order replacement, batch orders, referrals or public chart/orderbook feeds.

Cinder's SDK handles its own authentication and encryption. It is not a URL-only replacement for a venue's native client.

See [accounts](/guides/accounts), [risk and protection](/security/risk) and [recovery](/security/recovery) for the account rules.
