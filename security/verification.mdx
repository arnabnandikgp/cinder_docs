---
title: "Verification"
description: "Understand what attestation, account records and recovery proofs establish."
---

Different checks answer different questions. Verifying the enclave, verifying your claim and verifying the resources backing it are separate steps.

| Check | What it establishes | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enclave attestation and channel binding | Your connection is bound to the expected measured code and encryption key. | Error-free code, correct economics or continuous service availability. |
| Account records and reconciliation | Recorded balances and positions match the external evidence included in the reconciliation. | That a venue cannot fail or that assets are always accessible. |
| Recovery claim inclusion | Your final claim belongs to the published recovery root. | That the root includes every liability or that the vault contains enough assets. |
| Active funded recovery state | The program has an activated estate and backing that can be checked before a claim. | Automatic recovery from arbitrary open exposure or operator-free wind-down. |

The recovery manifest binds a final statement to the relevant deployment, pool, asset, settlement cutoff and policy. Your client checks that context before constructing a payment claim.

A Merkle proof is an inclusion proof, not a complete solvency proof. Complete backing also requires correctly recorded liabilities, appropriate asset valuation and access to the assets. Neither encryption nor a proof system can supply missing funds or venue withdrawal authority.

Cinder's private account data is not published as a public portfolio report. A public recovery claim reveals the information needed to verify and pay that claim, not the account's complete trading history.
