---
title: "Recovery"
description: "Claim final funds when the ordinary trading service is unavailable."
---

Ordinary withdrawals use the authenticated [payout](/api/methods/payout) workflow. Recovery is a separate, operator-assisted settlement process; service downtime alone does not make an old balance payable.

## Before claims open

The recovery operator stops competing writers and venue actions, reconciles uncertain operations, settles remaining exposure and returns accessible funds to the Solana custody vault.

Final claims are then derived from the settled account records. Authorized publication and activation make the funded recovery estate available for claims. A heartbeat timeout cannot skip these steps.

## Your recovery package

Your recipient-encrypted package contains your final claim, destination, previous-payment basis and the Merkle proof needed to verify inclusion in the published recovery root.

1. Obtain the published, signed recovery manifest and your privately delivered package locator.
2. Retrieve the encrypted package from a recovery store, independently of the ordinary trading API.
3. Decrypt it locally with your dedicated recovery key.
4. Verify the manifest, claim, active recovery state and vault backing.
5. Build and sign the claim with your owner wallet, then submit it to Solana.

The client verifies the configured payout destination; a package cannot redirect payment to an arbitrary recipient. Previous confirmed payouts are accounted for once, so they cannot be claimed again.

## Keep your recovery material

Retain your dedicated decryption key, package locator and a ciphertext backup. The decryption key is separate from the wallet signing key and is not recreated from a wallet signature. Losing it can prevent you from opening your package; automatic key replacement is not part of this recovery flow.

A saved ciphertext copy can be verified and decrypted when package stores are unavailable. If no valid copy can be obtained, a claim cannot be constructed from the public root alone.

## Limits

Recovery depends on operator action, venue access, complete settlement records and sufficient returned assets. It is not an immediate or unconditional exit from open venue positions.

The ordinary trading service is not needed to retrieve, decrypt and submit an activated final claim. The claim transaction and payment are public; your full private trading history is not.
