---
title: "Exact units"
description: "Use integer lots, price ticks and quote-asset atoms."
---

| Quantity | Representation |
| --- | --- |
| IDs, hashes and public keys | 32-byte `Uint8Array`; identifiers are nonzero. |
| Order or position quantity | Signed i64 `bigint` lots. |
| Prices and marks | u64 `bigint` ticks in the configured market unit. |
| Cash, basis, funding, fees and payouts | i128 `bigint` quote atoms, with field-specific sign and positivity rules. |
| Authority, deadlines and counts | u64 `bigint`; timestamps are milliseconds unless a field states otherwise. |
| Leverage | u64 `bigint` scaled by 10,000; `10_000n` means 1×. |
| Precision | A u32 market-unit version, not a number of decimal places. |
| Cursor | 40-byte `Uint8Array`. |
| Page revision | A 32-byte commitment. |

[Market metadata](/api/reads/markets) supplies the market and quote units and their exact rational conversion:

`signed fill value = lots × ticks × numerator / denominator`

The conversion must satisfy the market's exactness rules. Do not infer display decimals from the precision version.

Remaining basis is signed entry value, not a displayed average price. Settled cash excludes unsettled funding; accounting equity, risk equity and withdrawable funds are distinct. See the [account example](/guides/accounts).

Wire integers are big-endian and range-checked. Use `bigint` for SDK amounts rather than JavaScript `Number` or JSON numbers. Reference examples illustrate units and encoding, not current market prices or account limits.
