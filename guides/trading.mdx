An [order](/api/methods/order) sets the market, buy or sell direction, size, acceptable execution prices and fee limit. Time-in-force determines what happens to any unfilled quantity.

| Order type | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| GTC — good-til-cancelled | A limit order that can remain open until cancelled or completed. |
| ALO — add-liquidity-only | A post-only order: it must add resting liquidity rather than execute immediately as a taker. |
| IOC — immediate-or-cancel | Execute within the specified price bounds, then cancel any unfilled remainder. It may fill partially or not at all. |

Cinder records actual fills, not acknowledgements, as changes to your position. Repeated entries, partial closes and reversals update your remaining position and entry basis. Funding and fees are recorded separately.

**Entry basis** is the recorded entry value of the remaining position, not collateral paid to buy the underlying asset. It is used to calculate trading PnL. See [positions](/api/reads/positions) for a worked example.

The order command does not include stop-loss, take-profit or other trigger conditions. To change a resting order, cancel it, reconcile any fills and submit a new order; this is not an atomic replacement. Batch submission is not part of this command.

## Deadlines and cancellation

`goodUntil` is the latest time the order may be dispatched. It is **not** an automatic cancellation time for an already dispatched GTC or ALO order. Use [cancel](/api/methods/cancel) to stop a resting order.

Cancellation can race with execution. A cancellation acknowledgement does not rule out a fill that has already occurred or is still being reported. Check the original order's receipt for the reconciled result.

If delivery fails, keep the original request ID and [look up the operation](/api/methods/operation) before retrying. A new ID represents a new intent, not a continuation of the old request.

## Leverage and reduce-only

Choose leverage up to the market's maximum. Your selection controls private margin requirements; it does not create a separate native account or an isolated-margin position.

Reduce-only applies to **your Cinder position**: it must reduce that position without reversing it. The resulting trade can still change the shared venue account's margin requirement, so it remains subject to risk checks.
