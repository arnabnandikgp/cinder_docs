Cinder records your balance, positions, funding, fees and trading results separately from other customers. Orders execute through shared venue accounts, but your account is not a share of the combined pool.

## Four different amounts

| Amount | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Balance | Settled cash after deposits, realized trading results, settled funding, fees and payments. The API calls this `cash`. |
| Equity | Balance plus recognized unsettled funding and unrealized position PnL at the applicable marks. |
| Reserved funds | Amounts committed to pending orders, withdrawals or other operations. They cannot be spent a second time. |
| Withdrawable funds | The amount that can pass current margin, pending-operation, pool-risk and available-liquidity checks. It can be lower than equity or free collateral. |

Margin is the collateral required to support a position; it is not the position's full notional value. The API's `held` field reports explicit reservations, not the entire margin requirement.

## Worked example

Suppose you deposit $1,000, open a position and pay a $2 trading fee. The position has $40 of unrealized profit and $3 of recognized funding owed.

| Item | Amount |
| --- | ---: |
| Settled balance | $998 |
| Unrealized PnL | +$40 |
| Unsettled funding | −$3 |
| Accounting equity | $1,035 |
| Margin requirement | $200 |
| Separate reserved commitment | $100 |
| Illustrative free collateral | $735 |
| Immediately withdrawable after all checks | $600 |

Here, only $600 can be paid immediately because the remaining available value is not all accessible for payment. Closing or settling a position, completing a pending operation, or returning collateral from a venue can change that amount.

This example assumes the stated valuation and non-overlapping requirements. Actual risk checks may value collateral or unrealized gains more conservatively. Dollar amounts are illustrative; the API uses exact quote-asset atoms.

## Account access and pooled execution

Account setup supplies your private account ID, owner wallet and configured payout recipient. Connecting the wallet authenticates that account; it does not give you access to Cinder's venue keys.

Opposing customer positions can reduce Cinder's net venue exposure without eliminating each customer's position or obligations. Pooled execution therefore differs from a separate venue account in some stress scenarios. See [risk and protection](/security/risk).

Use [account reads](/api/reads/account) for balances and risk values, and [positions](/api/reads/positions) for position-level information.
